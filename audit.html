Audit Trail Integrity

Audit logs provide a chronological record of significant system actions. In the production system, audit records will be generated by the backend and protected against unauthorized modification.

Security-sensitive events such as fingerprint verification, unauthorized access attempts, custody transfers and device alerts should remain traceable to the responsible personnel, device and evidence record.
